ℹ️ Official Scoring & Randomiser Rules

1. Beat Proportional Points: 10 points are split proportionally based on catch: Points = 10 × (Angler Fish / Beat Total).
2. Blank Rule: An angler catching zero fish gets exactly 0.00 points for that session.
3. Unique Opponents: Every angler is randomized to face new opponents in each of the 4 sessions.
4. Odd Field 1v1v1 Guarantee: For 9 anglers, one beat per session is a 3-way match, ensuring every competitor participates in at least one 1v1v1.
